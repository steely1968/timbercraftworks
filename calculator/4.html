Plan view showing the roof joists, front and rear overhangs, and infill timber between joists.

Roof joists and infill timber
The joists are spaced evenly using the entered timber thickness. Their cut length is calculated using the horizontal roof width and the difference between the low and high roof heights, allowing for the roof slope. Infill timber is fitted between every pair of joists at both the front and rear. The diagram adapts to your measurements.

Roof Joist Cutting List

Includes all roof joists and the front and rear infill pieces.

Qty Timber Cut Length Thickness Position
Roof joists are cut to the calculated sloping length. The front and rear overhangs are included in the overall joist spacing layout. Infill pieces are cut to the clear gap between joists. Two infill pieces are allowed for every gap: one at the front and one at the rear. All infill timber uses the same thickness as the roof joists.
