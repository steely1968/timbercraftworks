Scaled Roof Joist Layout

Plan view showing the joists running front to back. Front and rear infills are positioned directly over the wall lines, inside the overhangs.

Roof joists and infill timber
The roof joists run continuously from front to back. They are spaced across the roof width. The front and rear infills connect adjacent joists across the gaps. Each infill is centred directly over its corresponding wall line. Overhangs extend beyond the wall lines, so the infills are not positioned at the ends of the overhanging joists.

Roof Joist Cutting List

Includes all front-to-back roof joists and the front and rear infill pieces.

Qty Timber Cut Length Thickness Position
Roof joists are cut to the calculated sloping length, including the front and rear overhangs. Infill pieces are cut to the clear gap between adjacent joists. Two infill pieces are allowed for every gap: one centred over the front wall line and one centred over the rear wall line. All infill timber uses the same thickness as the roof joists. The calculated sloping length assumes the roof pitch continues at the same angle through both overhangs. Check actual bearing and joint details before cutting timber.
