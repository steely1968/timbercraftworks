Door Settings

Door width is the clear opening between the inside faces of the two door frame studs. The frame therefore occupies the clear width plus two timber thicknesses.

Window Settings

Window width and height are clear opening dimensions. The sill measurement is taken upwards from the top surface of the bottom rail.

Front Wall Diagram

The drawing is a front elevation. Timber positions and dimensions are calculated in millimetres. The drawing is scaled to fit the page.

Timber Starting Positions

All horizontal positions are measured from the left-hand edge of the wall. All vertical positions are measured upwards from the bottom of the wall.

Vertical Stud Gap Measurements

Measurements are calculated using the actual starting positions of the vertical studs. Clear gap is measured from the right-hand face of one stud to the left-hand face of the next stud. Measurements are grouped by vertical framing height so that studs above and below openings are not incorrectly mixed with full-height studs.
