Construction layout: The bottom rail runs continuously across the complete front wall. The door opening sits on top of the bottom rail. The door header sits directly above the door opening. Two vertical cripple studs are fitted above the header, between the header and top rail. The window has horizontal framing directly above and below the opening. Vertical window cripple studs are fitted above and below the window opening. Main wall studs are distributed through the remaining solid wall sections. Clear gaps between studs are calculated and shown in the starting-position table. Door and window framing members are positioned around the openings. Only the front wall elevation is shown in the diagram.
